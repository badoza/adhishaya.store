Strength Rooted in Siddha Tradition

Thathuviruthi Chooranam is a proprietary Siddha herbal formulation developed by Adhishaya Herbs & Food using carefully selected traditional herbs that have been valued for generations in Siddha practice.

In today's fast-paced lifestyle, irregular food habits, stress, and physical exhaustion can affect overall energy. This formulation is designed to complement a healthy lifestyle by providing traditional herbal support for everyday vitality and men's wellness.

Key Wellness Benefits:

  • Supports normal functioning of the male reproductive system.
  • Helps maintain natural energy levels and vitality.
  • Supports muscular strength and physical performance.
  • Supports stamina and helps reduce daily fatigue.

Traditional Herbal Ingredients

Rather than relying on a single herb, this formulation combines multiple Siddha herbs that work together:

  • Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) One of the most respected herbs in traditional wellness systems. Traditionally valued for supporting physical stamina, vitality, and helping the body adapt to everyday stress.
  • Poonaikali Seeds (Mucuna pruriens) Traditionally valued in Siddha medicine for supporting reproductive wellness, vitality, and physical performance.
  • Nilappanai Kizhangu Appreciated as a nourishing herb in Siddha tradition for supporting stamina, strength, and everyday energy.
  • Moringa Seeds & Oorithal Thamarai Naturally contain beneficial plant compounds supporting antioxidant activity, immune wellness, and overall balance.
